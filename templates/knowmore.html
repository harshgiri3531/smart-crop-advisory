📘 Know More About NDVI & Crop Health

🌱 What is NDVI?

NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) is a key vegetation index used to measure the health and density of crops using satellite imagery.

NDVI values range from -1 to +1.

For agricultural applications, the useful NDVI range is generally 0.0 to +1.0.

🛰 How Does NDVI Work?

Healthy vegetation reflects more Near-Infrared (NIR) light and absorbs more Red light from the sun.

The formula for NDVI is:

NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)

Higher NDVI values indicate healthier and greener vegetation, while lower values show stressed crops or bare soil.

🌳 NDVI Value Interpretation

🟢 Healthy Crops: Good crop growth, proper irrigation, and sufficient nutrients.

🟡 Moderate Condition: Some stress in plants; check soil moisture, nutrient level, and pest presence.

🔴 Stressed Crops: Weak plant health; immediate irrigation, nutrients, and monitoring required.

📌 Why is NDVI Important?

  • Helps monitor crop health throughout the season
  • Detects stress early (water, nutrient deficiency, pests)
  • Improves resource use and reduces farming cost
  • Supports better yield prediction and decision-making
